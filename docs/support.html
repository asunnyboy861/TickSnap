Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a timer?

Tap any preset on the home screen to start a timer instantly. You can also tap the + button to create a custom timer with any duration.

Can I run multiple timers at once?

Yes! TickSnap supports multiple simultaneous timers. Each timer runs independently with its own countdown and alert.

What is Auto Repeat?

Auto Repeat automatically restarts a timer when it finishes. This is perfect for cooking (flip every 2 minutes), exercise intervals, or any repeating task.

Will timers work in the background?

Yes. TickSnap uses iOS local notifications to alert you even when the app is in the background or your screen is locked.

How do I change the alert sound?

Go to Settings (gear icon) and choose from 21 built-in alert sounds.

Is TickSnap really free?

Yes, TickSnap is completely free with no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases. All features are available to everyone.
